# Discord Channel Plan — What Each Channel Does For Us

## Philosophy

Discord isn't replacing iMessage. It's giving us **rooms for things that don't work well in a text thread** — automated feeds, searchable references, organized logistics. Nobody is forced to adopt it. Each channel earns its existence by solving a real problem.

**Who's on Discord right now:** Lesley, Greg, Cleo
**Who joins later (when there's a pull reason):** Genn, Crystal, morning nannies

---

## The Channels

### 📋 INFO — Always findable reference material

**Problem it solves:** "What's the pediatrician's number?" "What's the Wi-Fi password?" "What's the alarm code?" Currently this stuff is scattered across texts, sticky notes, and memory.

| Channel | What goes here | Why it's better than iMessage |
|---|---|---|
| **#emergency-contacts** | Pediatrician, poison control, urgent care, allergies, home address, school contacts | Pinned and always findable. No scrolling through months of texts. Nannies can find it instantly on a bad day. |
| **#house-rules** | Wi-Fi password, alarm codes, parking rules, delivery instructions, cleaning day (Monday), trash day | One place for the stuff you re-explain every time a new nanny starts. Update once, everyone sees it. |

**Who benefits:** Everyone, especially nannies. This is the channel that makes Crystal's onboarding worth it — "everything you need is pinned in #emergency-contacts and #house-rules."

---

### 🤖 CLEO — Automated feeds that would clutter iMessage

**Problem it solves:** Cleo's morning briefs, calendar reminders, and task updates are useful but noisy in a text thread. You can't mute one type of Cleo message on iMessage — it's all or nothing.

| Channel | What goes here | Why it's better than iMessage |
|---|---|---|
| **#daily-brief** | Cleo's morning briefing at 8am: today's calendar, nanny schedule, tasks due, weather | Read it when you want. It won't buzz your phone at 8am unless you choose to unmute it. Searchable — "what was on the calendar last Tuesday?" |
| **#alerts** | Calendar reminders (2hr and 30min before events), package deliveries, anything time-sensitive | These DO ping you. But only these. Separating alerts from the daily dump means you actually notice them. |
| **#cleo-commands** | Where you interact with Cleo: ask questions, run commands, get answers | Keeps the back-and-forth with Cleo out of #family-chat. You can have a 10-message conversation with Cleo about meal planning without anyone else seeing notification spam. |
| **#cleo-log** | Silent audit trail of everything Cleo does automatically | Muted by default. You never look at it unless something goes wrong and you want to see what Cleo did. Peace of mind, zero noise. |

**Who benefits:** Lesley and Greg. This is the core value prop of Discord — Cleo gets room to be useful without being annoying.

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 FAMILY — The group chat, but organized

**Problem it solves:** The iMessage group chat is one stream mixing "what should we do for dinner" with "the electrician is coming Thursday" with memes with "can you pick up Mira." Important things get buried.

| Channel | What goes here | Why it's better than iMessage |
|---|---|---|
| **#family-chat** | General conversation — the vibe of the iMessage group | Same energy, but now logistics have their own channels so this stays fun. |
| **#planning** | Vacations, big purchases, event planning, extended family coordination | Long-running conversations that would get lost in general chat. You can come back to "the Hawaii trip thread" weeks later. |
| **#random** | Memes, links, Titan photos, funny things the kids said | Guilt-free scrolling. Nothing important here, just vibes. |

**Who benefits:** Lesley, Greg, Genn (when she joins). This is the pull reason for Genn — "the family chat moved to Discord and we're planning the trip in #planning."

---

### 📅 HOUSEHOLD OPS — Logistics that need a home

**Problem it solves:** Grocery lists in Notes apps that nobody checks. Schedule changes communicated via individual texts. Maintenance requests that fall through the cracks.

| Channel | What goes here | Why it's better than iMessage |
|---|---|---|
| **#schedule** | Weekly nanny schedule (Cleo auto-posts Sunday evening), shift swaps, time-off requests | One source of truth. No more "wait, is Crystal coming Wednesday or did she swap?" Cleo keeps it updated. |
| **#grocery** | Shopping lists with checkboxes, Instacart coordination, "we're out of milk" | Shared, persistent list that doesn't disappear in a text thread. Cleo can build and manage it. Eventually: buttons to send to Instacart. |
| **#house-maintenance** | "Dishwasher is making a noise," plumber scheduling, cleaners Monday | Trackable. "When did we last call the HVAC guy?" is a search away. |
| **#meals** | Meal ideas, what's for dinner tonight, kids food prep notes | Replaces the "what should we eat" text chain that happens 5x/week. Cleo can suggest based on what's in the pantry. |

**Who benefits:** Lesley and Greg immediately. Crystal joins #schedule when it's consistently useful (pull, not push). Morning nannies might never need this if Crystal handles handoffs.

### 💰 PRIVATE — Just Lesley and Greg

**Problem it solves:** Financial conversations, sensitive topics, and couple-only chat that shouldn't be in the family group.

| Channel | What goes here | Why it's better than iMessage |
|---|---|---|
| **#finances** | Budget discussions, bills, investments, nanny payroll, tax stuff | Separated from everything else. Genn and nannies literally cannot see it (permissions, not honor system). Searchable — "what did we pay for the roof repair?" |
| **#private** | Couple-only conversation | For anything you'd text each other but not the group. |
| **#cleo-private** | Cleo commands involving sensitive info (financial lookups, etc.) | Keeps sensitive Cleo interactions out of the shared channels. |

**Who benefits:** Lesley and Greg. This is invisible to everyone else.

---

### 🏢 NANNY HUB — If/when nannies join

**Problem it solves:** Nannies texting Lesley individually for questions that could be answered once for everyone. No central place for announcements.

| Channel | What goes here | Why it's better than iMessage |
|---|---|---|
| **#announcements** | Family posts updates: schedule changes, house rules updates, holiday schedules | One-way (nannies can read, only family can post). Post once, all nannies see it. |
| **#questions** | Nannies ask questions, report issues | Any family member can answer. No more "Crystal texted me but I was driving, can you respond?" |
| **#kids-log** | Daily handoff notes: meals, naps, moods, activities, incidents | Persistent record. "How did Reya sleep this week?" — scroll back and check. Not buried in a text thread between two people. |

**Who benefits:** Crystal and the family. This is Crystal's pull reason — "post Mira's day in #kids-log instead of texting us separately. It's easier and everyone sees it." Morning nannies join only if the pattern is working.

## Adoption Plan

### Now: Lesley + Greg + Cleo

Set up all channels. Start using:
- **#daily-brief** — Cleo posts every morning (replaces iMessage morning brief)
- **#alerts** — Calendar reminders flow here
- **#grocery** — Next time you need groceries, use this instead of Notes
- **#cleo-commands** — Talk to Cleo here for anything complex
- **#finances** — Next financial discussion happens here
- **#schedule** — Cleo auto-posts the weekly nanny schedule

Everything else stays on iMessage for now. No announcement needed.

### Month 1: Natural pull moments

- **Genn:** "Hey, we're planning [thing] in #planning on Discord, come take a look" or "Titan photos are in #random now" — a reason to open the app, not a mandate
- **Crystal:** "Your schedule for next week is always in #schedule on Discord. Easier than me texting it each week." — one channel, one clear benefit

### Month 2+: Evaluate

- Is Discord reducing the "where did that info go?" problem?
- Are Cleo's automated feeds actually useful or just noise?
- Does Crystal check #schedule or does she still need a text?
- Does Genn engage or did she mute it?

Adjust based on what's actually working. Kill channels nobody uses. Add channels if a need emerges.

## What iMessage Still Does Best

| Scenario | Stay on iMessage |
|---|---|
| "Running 5 min late" | Yes — faster, no app switch |
| "Can you grab Mira?" | Yes — urgent, personal |
| Extended family (Kaitlin, etc.) | Yes — they won't join Discord |
| Cleo quick commands | Yes — "hey Cleo add milk to the list" via text still works |
| "I love you" | Yes — obviously |

Discord is for **structured, searchable, organized** household ops. iMessage is for **quick, personal, urgent** messages. Both coexist.
